Our vision

We want every conservation practice managing ecclesiastical buildings to have a workflow that matches the seriousness of the work — reducing admin, preserving institutional memory, and giving more time back to the buildings themselves.

Our story

A quinquennial inspection is often the first document anyone opens before maintenance, access works, reordering, redevelopment — or disposal.

Sorting a long list of defects by location and urgency still means juggling Word, Excel and Pages. Photographing every defect is usually the first thing cut for time. Churches receive pages of findings without knowing where the defects are, or how to locate a particular corbel, hood mould or tower louvre. Items get overlooked, then reappear five years later as costly repairs.

QI Resolved is built for inspectors. It aids the inspection on site, sorts the list of defects, and produces the report for the client.

We are talking to inspecting architects, surveyors and diocesan advisory committees about how it should work in practice — and building the denomination templates from what they tell us. If your practice would like to shape it, we would like to hear from you.

How it works

During the inspection

  1. Photograph the floor plan at the start.
  2. Photograph the defect in the app.
  3. Type the description.
  4. Pinpoint the defect on the plan.
  5. The app assigns a defect ID from the room.

After the inspection

  1. Re-shuffle the rooms into the right hierarchy.
  2. Group similar defects into a single item.
  3. Assign one cost range to the group.
  4. Generate the table of contents and export the PDF.
